The system lets a person, or an AI model acting for a person, **see the screen of a
computer and operate its keyboard and mouse remotely**, with nothing installed on that
computer. To the computer it looks like an ordinary keyboard and mouse. The operator can do
anything a person sitting at the keyboard could do: read private information, change
settings, send messages, make purchases, delete data, and enter passwords.

That makes it powerful and dangerous in the wrong hands or with the wrong instructions. You
